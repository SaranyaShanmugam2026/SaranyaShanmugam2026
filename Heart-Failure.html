Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing heart failure patient data to explore patient characteristics, clinical indicators, and health outcomes. Multiple healthcare datasets were combined and prepared for analysis. The project included descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analysis, along with an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

Project Objectives

  • Clean and prepare multiple healthcare datasets.
  • Explore patient demographics and clinical indicators.
  • Analyze patterns associated with patient outcomes.
  • Build predictive models to evaluate patient risk.
  • Present analytical results through a Streamlit dashboard.
